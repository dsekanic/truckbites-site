Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

We use submitted information to respond to inquiries, contact vendors, manage launch interest, improve our service, troubleshoot issues, and communicate TruckBites updates.

If you give permission, TruckBites may use publicly available or submitted vendor images for proof-of-concept mockups, promotional previews, app previews, website previews, or launch materials. Vendors may request removal by contacting us.

Third-Party Services

We may use services such as Formspree, Zoho, HubSpot, Supabase, GitHub Pages, or similar tools to process submissions, manage communications, operate our website, and support app functionality.

Data Protection

We take reasonable steps to protect submitted information, but no online service can guarantee complete security.

Your Choices

You may contact us to request updates, corrections, or removal of information you submitted.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact us at support@gettruckbites.com.
