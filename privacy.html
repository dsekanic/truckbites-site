Privacy Policy

Effective Date:5/28/2026

TruckBites LLC (“TruckBites,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect information when you use gettruckbites.com, the TruckBites mobile application, and related services.

1. Information We Collect

Information from Food Truck Vendors

Vendors may create accounts and provide information such as name, business name, email address, phone number, food truck details, menus, photos, specials, schedules, event participation, and live or scheduled location updates.

Information from App Users

General users are not required to create an account. We may collect limited information needed to provide app functionality, including device information, approximate or precise location if permission is granted, favorited trucks, app preferences, and interaction data.

Location Information

TruckBites may collect location information from users and vendors to show nearby food trucks, display vendor locations, improve search results, and provide location-based alerts. You may disable location access through your device settings, but some features may not work properly.

Communications and Notifications

If you opt in, we may send push notifications, emails, or other messages about food trucks, events, specials, app updates, and TruckBites announcements. You may opt out at any time through your device settings, unsubscribe links, or by contacting us.

2. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Operate, improve, and personalize TruckBites;
  • Show nearby food trucks and vendor locations;
  • Allow users to save or favorite trucks without requiring a full account;
  • Help vendors manage profiles, menus, photos, schedules, specials, and locations;
  • Send optional notifications and marketing communications;
  • Analyze app usage and improve performance;
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, misuse, or security issues;
  • Comply with legal obligations.

3. Favorites Without User Accounts

TruckBites may allow users to save favorite trucks without creating an account by storing preferences locally on the user’s device or by using a device-based identifier. If the app is deleted, device settings are changed, or local storage is cleared, favorites may be lost.

4. User Reviews and Content

If ratings, reviews, photos, comments, or other content features are available, the content you submit may be visible to other users. Do not submit private, confidential, unlawful, offensive, or misleading content.

5. How We Share Information

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with service providers that help us operate TruckBites, including hosting, analytics, maps, notifications, email, customer support, and security providers. We may also share information when required by law, to protect our rights, or during a business transfer such as a merger, acquisition, or sale.

6. Third-Party Services

TruckBites may use third-party services such as app stores, maps, analytics providers, hosting providers, email platforms, notification services, and social media platforms. These third parties may collect information according to their own privacy policies.

7. Data Retention

We keep information for as long as reasonably necessary to operate TruckBites, provide services, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and comply with legal obligations. Vendor account information may be retained while the account is active and for a reasonable period afterward.

8. Your Choices

You may:

  • Disable location access through your device settings;
  • Opt out of push notifications through your device settings;
  • Unsubscribe from marketing emails using the unsubscribe link;
  • Request access, correction, or deletion of your information by contacting us;
  • Delete or clear locally stored favorites through app or device settings when available.

9. Children’s Privacy

TruckBites is not intended to collect personal information from children under 13 without appropriate consent. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13 without required consent, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

10. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Indiana and United States Users

TruckBites LLC is based in Indiana, United States. By using TruckBites, you understand that your information may be processed in the United States.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Continued use of TruckBites after updates means you accept the revised policy.
