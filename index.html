Founder note

Built by people who want local food trucks to win.

TruckBites was created in Southern Indiana after seeing how difficult it can be for customers to know where their favorite food trucks are — and how much opportunity vendors lose when customers cannot find them at the right time.

This is being built from real local food truck experiences, not from a generic template. We are eating locally, following trucks, watching the pain points, and building a tool that helps both vendors and customers.

Common questions

Is TruckBites live yet?

TruckBites is preparing for a July 2026 launch push. We are currently recruiting Founding Vendors before public launch.

Who should sign up first?

Food truck owners, mobile vendors, pop-ups, and local food businesses that want more visibility should join as Founding Vendors.

Do customers need an account?

The goal is to make discovery easy for customers. Vendor accounts are the priority; customer login requirements will be kept as simple as possible.

What does it cost vendors?

Vendors who join before October 31 receive free access through launch. Memberships begin November 1 at $49/month or $500/year.

What areas are included?

Vendors from any location are welcome to join TruckBites. Early vendor interest helps determine where TruckBites expands next.

How do vendors get started?

Vendors can start by completing the Founding Vendor signup form at gettruckbites.com/join.
